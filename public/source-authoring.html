Build from the studies themselves

Use a researcher guide, template or existing draft to retrieve relevant source wording, order the extracts and create a rough linked manuscript. Extract wording is locked; only the connecting text is editable.

The workflow first maps substantive passages by research function, then aligns evidence to the researcher’s claim or section. Article titles, bylines, page furniture and reference lists are never treated as evidence.
