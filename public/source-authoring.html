3. Extract, order and link

Local evidence mapping filters non-substantive material and identifies problem, theory, method, finding, boundary and implication passages. Guided selection spends at most one provider call to judge which exact candidates genuinely support, contrast with or qualify the draft claim; it may abstain, but it may never rewrite an extract.

Preflight will report manuscript size, visible lines, citation groups and confirmed source identities before processing.

Build a source-led synthesis

Use this when several studies must become one argument rather than a chain of pasted extracts. The system recovers the author’s intended position, creates source-linked notes, compares the evidence and then composes. This route does not pass through the Editor’s preservation gates.

All citations are inserted from researcher-confirmed source identities. Any retained source wording is exact-checked and visibly quoted; failed checks are reported without erasing the completed draft.
